# Paint By Paddle

A one-thumb arcade game for iPhone: bounce a ball through the gaps in the walls and paint a pixel-art picture, brick by brick.

- [Support](support): how to play, pause, settings, sound in Silent Mode, contact
- [Privacy policy](privacy): the game collects no data
